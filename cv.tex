\section*{Research Experience}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}Xr@{}}
\textbf{Tool-Centric Planning for Dexterous Tool Use} & Mar. 2026 - Jul. 2026
\end{tabularx}
\begin{itemize}
\item Formulated dexterous tool use as tool-centric functional goal-trajectory generation, representing manipulation tasks through functional tool geometry and tool-environment interaction constraints.
\item Developed a VLM-grounded geometric planning pipeline that grounds functional tool parts and interaction targets from language, tool geometry, and RGB-D observations, then compiles them into staged SE(3) tool-pose goals for execution by a pretrained goal-tracking policy.
\end{itemize}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}Xr@{}}
\textbf{Non-Line-of-Sight Reconstruction via Target-Domain Latent Alignment} & Aug. 2025 - Nov. 2025
\end{tabularx}
\begin{itemize}
\item Formulated severely degraded NLOS reconstruction as a target-domain latent alignment problem, using representations learned from clean target images as a structural prior for recovering information obscured by diffuse light transport.
\item Decoupled target representation learning from measurement inversion by first learning a compact target latent space and its decoder, then training the NLOS encoder to align measurements with this latent space through coupled latent- and image-space objectives.
\end{itemize}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}Xr@{}}
\textbf{PinNet: Edge-Optimized Skin Lesion Segmentation} & Jul. 2024 - Dec. 2024
\end{tabularx}
\begin{itemize}
\item Designed an ultra-lightweight segmentation architecture around the boundary ambiguity and multi-scale appearance variation of skin lesions, combining morpho-spectral feature extraction with dual-perspective attention to strengthen edge-sensitive and contextual representations.
\item Reduced the model to approximately 0.05M parameters and 0.068 GFLOPs while maintaining competitive segmentation performance, targeting deployment under strict computation and memory constraints.
\end{itemize}
